# Competitive analysis: Unitree G1 and Agibot X2

## Evidence and limits
Fresh Google rental-query results were read via the owner's research Chrome: `serp-remote-g1.json`, `serp-remote-x2.json`. This is that browser's localised/personalised SERP snapshot, not a claim of nationwide rankings; G1 results include a Yekaterinburg Divent page. Ignore AI Overview as factual evidence. Own KIBER/Cyber Gosha results, social/video results and purchase-only pages are not independent rental competitors.

Remote page reads: Divent G1, ROBORT G1, Roboagency X2, Rent Robot X2 Ultra. Additional direct full-text reads: Rent Robot G1, SBE Robotics X2 Ultra, Bots-4-Events X2 Ultra. Manufacturer references: Unitree G1/G1 EDU and AGIBOT X2. X2 Ultra competitor propositions describe adjacent configurations and do not establish the features of KIBER's X2.

## Unitree G1
- **Divent** (`competitor-g1-divent.json`): separates guest benefits, brand benefits, included equipment and additional options; prompt preparation, custom scenarios and branded clothing are separately presented. Good buyer-oriented structure. Our response: explain the agreed repertoire, audience interaction and included versus additional preparation. Do not copy claims of guaranteed viral reach, acrobatics or an answer within ten minutes.
- **ROBORT** (`competitor-g1-robort.json`): describes motion and configuration differences, gives a rental rate, photos/video and an educational project. Rental explanation is mixed with product purchase cards; main text says 130 cm while a product card says 132 cm. Our response: keep rental intent clear and use manufacturer figures only for the verified configuration, not competitor rounded values. Distinguish EDU development features from a prepared event programme.
- **Rent Robot** (`source-0.json`): explicit model/features, event media, use cases and a package labelled rental/delivery/training. Voice AI and free movement are broadly promised. Our response: specify operator/prepared actions/voice scenario separately; battery duration is not event coverage. Do not substitute their service package or tariff for KIBER's.

## Agibot X2
- **Roboagency** (`competitor-x2-roboagency.json`): extensive scenarios, surface/network/noise requirements and logistics. However, the same page uses 6.8 km/h in prose and 6.8 m/s in its parameter panel, and mixes broad AI/platform claims with an event offer. Our response: retain practical site questions, verify edition independently, exclude unconfirmed speed, emotion recognition, spontaneous learning and acrobatics.
- **Rent Robot X2 Ultra** (`competitor-x2-rentrobot.json`): clear edition/name, media, concise specifications, branding and rental package. Our response: do not transfer Ultra weight, DOF or equipment into the X2 card. Replace generic lists of occasions with a concrete guest action and a preparation condition for each scenario.
- **SBE Robotics** (`sbe-x2.json`): strong focus on organiser tasks, prepared scenarios and operator support. Operator presence is emphasised, while a later conditions block qualifies technical support as provided when needed. Our response: state the actual agreed service scope without conflicting wording or unverified minimum rental duration.
- **Bots-4-Events** (`bots-x2.json`): explains booth attraction, sponsor/photo moments and conference breaks in guest-oriented language. This is an international Ultra offer, not local pricing proof. Our response: describe the attendee experience plainly but avoid blanket promises of suitability for every venue or guaranteed performance.

## Decisions for page blocks
- Introduction: model-specific rental offer and tangible guest interaction, not AI terminology or hardware catalogue copy.
- Capabilities: pair each of six capability images with its actual topic; give dimensions/configuration where verified and label demonstrations versus configured functions. Keep Hero and both galleries frozen.
- Scenarios: specific guest action, organiser purpose and relevant preparation constraint. Do not fill all models with the same six generic paragraphs.
- FAQ G1: repertoire/voice, operator, Basic versus EDU, surface and space, battery versus programme, children/distance, branding, scope of quotation.
- FAQ X2: exact edition, interaction and voice conditions, prepared dance/movement, noise/connectivity, floor/space, programme and pauses, branding, what is actually included.
- Related articles: use only approved canonical article titles/covers; G1/X2 comparison is directly relevant to both. Select other approved articles by model/scenario, not to fill every slot.
- Related robots: closest alternatives from the approved humanoid collection; no self-recommendation. Overlap between relevant sets is acceptable.

## Remaining factual guard
Research is sufficient to begin the content pass. Exact owned-unit configurations and any custom voice/choreography capabilities must be reconciled with project facts; unsupported claims are omitted rather than filled from competitors. No automatic changes to approved articles, Hero, galleries, prices or live routing.
